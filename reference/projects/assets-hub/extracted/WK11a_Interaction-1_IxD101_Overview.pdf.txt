HW10: Create an Interactive Prototype
KEY OBJECTIVE(S)
● Put together a digital clickable/interactive prototype (create hotspot interactions in Figma). Base 
designs on wireframes/wireﬂows and related feedback. Push toward high ﬁdelity screens in 
prototypes with logical ﬂow and interactions supporting the project concept.
ASSIGNMENT
1. Create a  design system  that supports your concept. Leverage Figma UI kits as needed. 
2. Look to your wireframes/wireﬂows, user ﬂow, and feature prioritization to determine what screens to 
build and ﬁt together as an interactive prototype.
3. For each screen, consider the interactions and decisions a user can choose from in order to move them 
closer to their goal. What is the priority? Does the design express the ‘big idea’/UX Vision/HMW?
DELIVERABLE
Upload a prototype link to Canvas. Be prepared to share during our next class.
