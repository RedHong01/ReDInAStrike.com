HW9: (individual) Create Wireframes/Wireﬂows
KEY OBJECTIVE(S)
● Design and deliver wireframes/wireﬂows for your solo project. I recommend beginning by sketching 
on paper before moving digital. The deliverable is a link to Figma screen designs (wireframes).
ASSIGNMENT
1. Leverage your user ﬂow and key features. Determine what screens and features to wireframe and how 
they ﬁt together and ﬂow.
2. Design and deliver mid-ﬁdelity wireframes/wireﬂows. Consider best practices re: Usability Heuristics 
where applicable.
3. For each wireframe, consider the interactions and decisions a user can choose from in order to move 
them closer to their goal. 
○ What is the priority? Does the design express the ‘big idea’/UX Vision/HMW?
○ What is the key features or interactions are being expressed?
○ Provide annotations where helpful to provide user and ecosystem context
DELIVERABLE
Share a link to your work in Canvas. Be prepared to share your work in class, however, you do not need to 
print your work to pin up.
