Horizontal Progression
Players expand their capabilities or options
● New Playstyles or Abilities
● Customization and Variety
● Exploration and Content Expansion
● New Options Balanced with Existing Ones
Benefits:
● Promotes Strategic Thinking ,  Exploration , 
Creativity
● Avoids Power Creep
Final Fantasy  Spell System
● Enhances Replayability
Risks:
● Analysis Paralysis
11
=== page 12 ===
Vertical Progression
Players advance by becoming progressively 
stronger or more powerful
● Stat Increases
● Stronger Gear or Upgrades
● Higher Difficulty Tiers
● Linear Progression Path
Benefits:
● Clear Sense of Growth
● Structured Goals
God of War  Weapon Upgrades
● Accessible Design
Risks
● Power Creep
12
=== page 13 ===
Cyclical Progression
Players repeatedly go through a loop or cycle of 
growth, reset, and re-growth, often with some form 
of permanent advancement between cycles
● Temporary and Permanent Progress
● Increasing Complexity or Challenge
● Partial Reset of Progress
● Repetition with Growth
Benefits:
● Grow
Cookie Clicker  Prestige Loop
● Player Skill Development
● Game Mastery
Risks
● Slow Long-Term Progress

● Role-Playing

Economic Progression
Players advance by accumulating, managing, and 
spending resources, currencies, or commodities.
● Acquisition Methods
● Spending/Investements
● Balancing Supply and Demand
● Managing Inflation and Scaling Costs
Benefits:
● Encourages Exploration and Trading
● Strategic Decision-Making
● Alternate Progression Paths 
Age of Empires  Villager System
Risks
● Micromanagement

Mission Progression
Completing specific tasks or objectives, usually 
organized into a sequence, to advance the game’s 
story or gameplay experience
● Structured Objectives
● Narrative Advancement
● Unlocking Content
● Mission Variety
Benefits:
● Engagement Through Clear Goals
Grand Theft Auto V  Mission System
● Immersion Through Narrative
● Balances Difficulty Through Mission Challenge
Risks
● Repetitive or Monotonous Missions
18
=== page 19 ===
Level Progression
Advancing through a series of distinct levels or stages, 
each with its own challenges, layout, or mechanics.
● Linear or Branching Structure
● Increasing Challenge
● Thematic Variety
● Replayablity
Benefits:
● Clear Sense of Progress
● Gradually Introduce New Mechanics and Enemies
● Replayability For Better Performance or Missed 
Super Mario Bros.  Level System
Content
Risks
● Unbalanced Length, Difficulty or Complexity

World Progression
Unlocking and exploring new areas of a game’s world as 
players advance.
● Exploration-Based Progression
● Dynamic World Changes
● Gameplay Affects Access and Closures
● Sense of Scale
Benefits:
● Encourages Exploration and Discovery
● Enhances Immersion
● Provides Player Freedom and Agency
Breath of the Wild  Shrine Map
Risks
● Overwhelming Scope and Unclear Navigation
● Arbitrary Gatekeeping for Progression

Narrative Progression
How a game’s plot, characters, and themes unfold as 
players interact with the game world.
● Player Choice and Consequences
● Linear vs. Nonlinear Storytelling
● Integration with Gameplay Mechanics
● Dialogue and Cut Scenes
Benefits:
● Emotional Engagement
● Motivation to Progress
● Immersion in the World
Doki Doki Literature Club!  Dialogue 
System
Risks
● Limited Player Agency
● Overloading Player with Lore
22
=== page 23 ===
Time-Based Progression
Players are reinforced and rewarded for actual time spent 
playing in the game
● Real-time Dependency
● Reinfocement for Active Play
● Incorporates Passive Progression
● Encourages Routine Play
Benefits:
● Player Retention and Engagement
● Anticipation and Accomplishment
● Balance Between Active and Casual Play
Animal Crossing  Timed Event 
System
Risks
● Impatience and Abandoment From Timers
● Daily Routines May Feel Like Chores

=== page 24 ===
Luck-Based Progression
Mechanics where progression or rewards depend partly 
or entirely on chance
● Random Loop Drops or Quest Rewards
● Loot Boxes and Gacha Systems
● Crafting or Upgrade Random Outcomes
● Procedural Generation
Benefits:
● Excitement and Anticipation
● Surprise and Variety
● Long-Term Engagement
Overwatch  Loot Box System
Risks
● Player Frustration with Randomness
● Monetization Controversy
