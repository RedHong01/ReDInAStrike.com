Semi-Rapid Prototyping
1. Form a plan (What is the story your prototype tells?)
○ What is the entry point?
○ Where and and what your key features or “big idea”?
○ What is the end result?
2. Organize your resources
○ Select a tool, determine device to prototype for, ﬁnd UI kits
3. Build the screens of your prototype (high ﬁdelity)
○ Arrange UI elements, colors, fonts, etc. for screens
4. Get interactive and ﬂow
○ Determine and implement relevant interactions (transitions, linking
screens via hotspots, etc.)

HW5: (group) Create an Interactive Prototype
KEY OBJECTIVE(S)
● Each group will put together a digital clickable/interactive prototype. Base designs on individual 
wireframes/wireﬂows and related feedback. Push to high-ﬁdelity screens in the prototypes.
ASSIGNMENT
1. Choose a design tool that everyone in your group will use to create designs (Figma or Adobe XD). 
Leverage UI kits for that tool where applicable. (such as those found  here )
2. Look to your wireframes/wireﬂows, user ﬂow, and feature prioritization to determine what screens to 
build and ﬁt together as an interactive prototype.
3. Help each other and collaborate on design ideas. Each group member should be involved in creating  the 
interactive prototype.
4. For each screen, consider the interactions and decisions a user can choose from in order to move them 
closer to their goal. What is the priority? Does the design express the ‘big idea’/UX Vision/HMW?
DELIVERABLE
Upload a prototype link to Canvas. Be sure permissions are set so that anyone can view. Be prepared to 
share during our next class.
