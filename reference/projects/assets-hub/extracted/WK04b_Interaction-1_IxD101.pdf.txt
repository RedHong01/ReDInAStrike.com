Good Navigation 
● Supports  the top  tasks  for the goal that users came for. 
● Avoids  unnecessarily  vague  or creative terminology. 
● Is  persistent  and  consistent  throughout the site. 
● Is  clearly labeled  and uses  familiar conventions . 
● Is  comprehensive , yet  concise .
● Should not disappear, change, or move  without clear cause. 
● Supports a users ability  capture the scope  of the site thru its 
primary navigation links.

HW4: (individual) Sketch Wireframes/Wireﬂows
KEY OBJECTIVE(S)
● Each team member is responsible for designing and delivering wireframes/wireﬂows. It is critical for 
groups to meet before starting to make a plan. I recommend sketching on paper for this assignment.
ASSIGNMENT
1. Look to your user ﬂow and feature prioritization to determine what screens and features to wireframe 
and how they ﬁt together and map to your user ﬂow.
2. You may help each other and collaborate on design ideas, however, each student is responsible for 
designing and delivering their own set of mid-ﬁdelity annotated wireframes/wireﬂows.
3. For each wireframe, consider the interactions and decisions a user can choose from in order to move 
them closer to their goal. 
○ What is the priority? Does the design express the ‘big idea’/UX Vision/HMW?
○ Provide annotations where applicable
4. Sketch large enough and on as many sheets of paper (11x17 recommended) as needed to make your 
designs clear. Be prepared to pin up your designs for crit/feedback next class (bring wall safe tape!).
DELIVERABLE
Upload clearly documented images to Canvas. Print your work to pin up and share during our next class.
