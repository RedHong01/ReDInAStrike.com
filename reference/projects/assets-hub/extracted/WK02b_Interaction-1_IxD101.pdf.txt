HMW “How might we…”
Let’s break down the language:
● HOW: assumes solutions exist or can be designed
● MIGHT:  acknowledges  some of the ideas may work, and others may not
● WE: communicates approaching a challenge together, as a team

Why Do We Make Personas?
● To remind us of who the user is. 
● To summarize user research. 
● To focus design efforts on solving the “right” problem. 
● To make design less subjective. 
● To help us stay focused on the user. 
● To  create EMPATHY  for the user.
=== page 17 ===
What Do Personas Typically Include?
● A name and photo
● Demographic information
● Goals and needs (Pain points)
● Preferences
● A brief description of that user’s ideal experience
=== page 18 ===
Provisional Personas (Proto Personas)
● Useful as a starting point
● They are often temporary
● It is a placeholder
● Used as a communication tool
● They  need to be validated  through discovery (user interviews)

HW: (group) Personas
KEY OBJECTIVE(S)
● Create a provisional persona based on your group assumptions and preliminary research. After conducting interviews, 
create a second, updated persona rooted in ﬁndings and insights.
ASSIGNMENT
1. Open the Mural Persona Grid template at:  https://www.mural.co/templates/persona-grid
2. Each group, complete one persona on the template
○ Focus your personas on what you assume is critical for summarizing your customer/user group needs, and how 
they currently deal with your stated problem.
○ Consider the speciﬁc deﬁned location, proposed audience, and your three HMW statements to help inform the 
personas.
○ Complete the three sections for your persona (outlined on the previous slide)
3. Keep this provisional persona in mind when screening participants for interviewing.  After conducting the interviews, 
create a second, updated persona based on ﬁndings and insights.

User Interviews:
Recruiting
=== page 27 ===
How to Recruit
● Aim for participants that represent the actual target audience 
(based on screeners). 
● Screeners are short surveys (typically a set of 1-3 questions) that 
qualify potential participants to make sure you meet your 
recruiting goals. 
● Consider, “what is the criteria for which I am recruiting?”. Your 
group has control over the criteria for recruitment, however have 
a reason for what you select, and be prepared to explain why.

=== page 43 ===
Interviews Can Help Clarify
Practice active listening:
● WHO  is your user?
● WHAT  is their problem / need / experience.
● WHEN  does it occur?
● WHERE  does it occur?
● WHY  does it occur? (peel back layers with 5 Whys)
=== page 44 ===
Documenting Interviews
Immediately following each interview capture:
● What was the most  memorable or surprising
story you heard during your interview?
● What did this participant seem to care about 
the most? (What  motivates  them?)
● What seemed to  frustrate  the participant? 
(Pain points)
● What questions would you like to explore in a 
future  conversation?
