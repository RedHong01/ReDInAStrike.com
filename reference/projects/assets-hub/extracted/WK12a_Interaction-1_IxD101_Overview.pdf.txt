HW11: Test and Update Prototypes
KEY OBJECTIVE(S)
● Each student will test their prototype and push forward the design and logic of the prototypes.
ASSIGNMENT
1. Test your prototype with people outside of our class.
○ What did users like?
○ What was confusing?
○ What design opportunities might this feedback lead to?
2. Make note of the feedback. (To update your designs and to include in your process book)
3. Update your prototypes!
DELIVERABLE
Upload a prototype link to Canvas. Be prepared to share during our next class.
